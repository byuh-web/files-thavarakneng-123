Stop 5: Central Market

Central Market is a busy market in Phnom Penh. People can find clothes, food, gifts, jewelry, and many other items there. This stop shows daily life and shopping in the city.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because it shows another side of Cambodia, not only temples but also modern city life.
