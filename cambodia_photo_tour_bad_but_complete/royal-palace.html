Stop 4: Royal Palace

The Royal Palace is in Phnom Penh. It has beautiful buildings and is connected to Cambodian royal history. The area is clean, bright, and important for visitors who want to learn about the capital city.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because it connects the tour to Phnom Penh and Cambodian royal culture.
