Stop 2: Bayon Temple

Bayon Temple is located in the Angkor area. It is famous for its large stone faces. The faces make the temple easy to recognize and very interesting to visitors.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because the stone faces show the special art and style of the Angkor period.
