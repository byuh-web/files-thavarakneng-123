Welcome

This website is a photo tour of Cambodia. It shows five places that are important for history, culture, religion, and daily life. Each place page has pictures and a short explanation.

Tour Places

  • Angkor Wat - a famous temple and national symbol.
  • Bayon Temple - a temple with large stone faces.
  • Ta Prohm - an old temple with trees growing around it.
  • Royal Palace - an important royal place in Phnom Penh.
  • Central Market - a busy market in the city.

The design uses one repeating background image and one decorative background image from the CSS file. The list above also uses a custom image bullet.
