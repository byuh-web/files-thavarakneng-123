Stop 1: Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat is one of the most famous places in Cambodia. It is known for its tall towers, long walkways, and detailed stone carvings. Many visitors come here because it shows the beauty of Khmer architecture.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because Angkor Wat is a symbol of Cambodia and appears on the Cambodian flag.
