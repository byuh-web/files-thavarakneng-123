Stop 1: Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat is one of the most famous places in Cambodia. It is known for its large temple towers, long walkways, and detailed stone carvings. Many people visit this place because it shows the strength and beauty of Khmer architecture.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because Angkor Wat is connected to Cambodian identity. It also appears on the Cambodian flag and is a strong symbol of the country's history.
