Stop 2: Bayon Temple

Bayon Temple is located in the Angkor area. It is easy to recognize because of the many calm stone faces carved into the towers. The temple feels peaceful and mysterious at the same time.

Why This Stop Matters

Bayon Temple is important because its carvings show religion, art, and power during the Angkor period. The faces make the temple one of the most memorable places in Cambodia.
