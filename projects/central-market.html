Stop 5: Central Market

Central Market, also called Psar Thmei, is a well-known market in Phnom Penh. It is a place where people can buy food, clothes, jewelry, souvenirs, and many local products. This stop shows daily city life in Cambodia.

Why This Stop Matters

Central Market is important because it shows the energy of Phnom Penh. It is not only a place for shopping, but also a place where visitors can see local culture and everyday business.
