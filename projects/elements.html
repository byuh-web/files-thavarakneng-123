A Lion's Life

Life in the Pride

A lion lives in a group called a pride. Lions work together to protect their territory, raise their cubs, and find food. Strength is important, but teamwork helps the pride survive. Family is a major part of a lion's life.


Lions spend much of the day resting because they need energy for hunting. My lion story © Thavarak Neng. A lion may look calm during the day, but it becomes powerful and focused when it is time to hunt.

“The lion survives through courage, patience, and the strength of the pride.”
