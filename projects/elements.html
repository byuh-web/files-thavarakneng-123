A Lion's Life

The King of the Savannah

Lions are known as the kings of the animal kingdom because of their power and leadership. They live mostly in Africa and spend much of their time resting under the hot sun.

A lion usually lives in a group called a pride. The pride works together to protect territory and care for cubs. Teamwork helps lions survive in the wild.

Female lions are the main hunters of the pride. They hunt animals such as zebras and antelopes during the night because the weather is cooler and hunting becomes easier.

Male lions protect the pride from danger. Their loud roar can be heard up to 5 miles away. A lion's roar shows strength and warns other animals to stay away.

Lion cubs are playful and curious when they are young. They learn survival skills from older lions as they grow stronger each year.

Lions are important to nature because they help control animal populations. Without lions, the balance of the ecosystem could change greatly.


“Lions are the only cats that live in groups called prides.”
