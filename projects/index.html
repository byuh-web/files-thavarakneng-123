Welcome to the Cambodia Photo Tour

This website gives a short photo tour of Cambodia. The tour focuses on five places that show Cambodia's history, religion, architecture, and daily life. Each stop has photos and a short explanation so visitors can understand why the place is important.

Tour Stops

  • Angkor Wat - Cambodia's most famous temple and national symbol.
  • Bayon Temple - a temple known for its large stone faces.
  • Ta Prohm - a jungle temple where trees grow around ancient walls.
  • Royal Palace - a beautiful royal site in Phnom Penh.
  • Central Market - a busy market that shows city life and local trade.

About the Design

The website uses one repeated background image and one decorative non-repeating background image through CSS. It also uses a custom image bullet for the tour list.
