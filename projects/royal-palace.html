Stop 4: Royal Palace

The Royal Palace is located in Phnom Penh. It has bright colors, traditional roofs, and beautiful gardens. The palace shows Cambodia's royal history and formal architecture.

Why This Stop Matters

This stop matters because the Royal Palace connects modern Phnom Penh with the country's traditions, ceremonies, and national history.
