Stop 3: Ta Prohm

Ta Prohm is famous because nature and history meet in one place. Large tree roots grow over the stone walls, making the temple look like it is part of the jungle. This makes the place very different from many other temples.

Why This Stop Matters

Ta Prohm shows how nature can change a historical place over time. It also helps visitors imagine how ancient buildings can become part of the forest.
