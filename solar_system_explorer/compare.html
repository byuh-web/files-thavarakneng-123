Why comparing worlds helps

Planet comparison helps beginners see patterns. Some objects are rocky, some are gas giants, and some are moons instead of planets. NASA lists eight planets in the solar system and explains that their sizes, distances, and features vary widely. NASA About the Planets

The table below is new for this assignment. It summarizes the worlds discussed on the website in simple student language rather than copying a table from another website.

Selected Solar System Worlds
World Category Known For Beginner Observation Idea

Saturn Gas giant planet Large ring system Use a telescope to see the ring shape
